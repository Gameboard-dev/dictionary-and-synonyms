PART 3. SOURCES OF THE WORDS

The plugin ships no dictionary, thesaurus or encyclopedia data. It reads the sources below over the network when you
select a word, or downloads a whole dictionary when you press Download in its settings, and keeps what it finds on
your own computer: in a note in your vault, whose `sources` property names each source and its licence, and, for a
downloaded dictionary, in your computer's cache folder. These are the sources, their licences and the attribution they
ask for. Text you keep from them stays under their licences, whatever you do with it later.

================================================================================
Wiktionary (English Wiktionary, https://en.wiktionary.org/), definitions and synonyms
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Read page by page through the MediaWiki Action API (https://en.wiktionary.org/w/api.php).
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); much of the text is also available under the GNU Free Documentation
License (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl-1.3.html). Terms: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use.
Attribution: each note links the Wiktionary page its definitions came from, whose history lists the authors.
Requests identify the plugin with a User-Agent, as Wikimedia's User-Agent policy asks
(https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Wikimedia_Foundation_User-Agent_Policy).

================================================================================
Wikipedia (https://www.wikipedia.org/), the opening sentences of an article for a term no dictionary has
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Read through the MediaWiki Action API with the TextExtracts extension (https://<language>.wikipedia.org/w/api.php).
Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and the GNU Free Documentation License where
the article offers it. Attribution: each note keeps the article's title and URL (`summary-title`, `summary-url`).

================================================================================
Datamuse API (https://www.datamuse.com/api/), synonyms, and short definitions when Wikimedia cannot answer
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wiktextract's extract of Wiktionary, by kaikki.org (https://kaikki.org/dictionary/rawdata.html), downloaded only when you
press Download
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The file raw-wiktextract-data.jsonl.gz: every entry of English Wiktionary, extracted by Wiktextract
(https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract); the plugin keeps the selected language's entries. The text is Wiktionary's,
under CC BY-SA 4.0 and the GNU Free Documentation License as above. kaikki.org asks academic users to cite
Tatu Ylonen, "Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data", Proceedings of the 13th Conference on
Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC 2022), pp. 1317-1325, and appreciates a link to https://kaikki.org/.
Attribution: each note made from it links the word's Wiktionary page.
